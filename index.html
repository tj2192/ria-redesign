What I don’t know

One thing worth mentioning: this is an outside-in redesign. I’m looking at Ria from the user’s side, without knowing the decisions and constraints behind the current experience.

I don’t know HealthifyMe’s technical architecture, how reliably Ria can interpret and update food records, the limitations of its nutrition database, or the cost of running more complex AI interactions. I also haven’t accounted for the legal and regulatory considerations around personalized nutrition advice, or the business priorities the team is working towards.

I’m sure the team has explored many of these problems already. Some of the decisions I’ve questioned may be deliberate trade-offs.

So I’m not presenting this as a better solution, but as an exploration of what the experience could look like with fewer conversational steps and more direct interaction.

I’d be interested to understand what constraints shaped the existing design.
